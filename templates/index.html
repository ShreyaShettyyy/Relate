About Relate

Relate is an e-commerce platform that learns from customer purchase patterns to recommend useful products and accessories.

How It Works

Personal History

If you usually buy two items together, the store remembers and suggests them when you view either item.

Community Purchases

When multiple customers purchase items together, other shoppers see them under Frequently Bought Together.

Useful Products

If no one has bought an item with another yet, the system suggests useful accessories like a phone case for a phone.

Live Learning

Every completed order is added to the system to continuously improve recommendations.

Recommendation Methods

Market Basket Analysis (Apriori)

Analyzes customer shopping carts to find products that are frequently bought together in the same order.

Purchase Connection Metrics

Rules measure how strong a product combination is:

Support
How often the product combination is purchased.
Confidence
How likely a customer is to buy item B when buying item A.
Lift
Measures if two products are genuinely bought together more than by random chance.

Useful Accessory Matching

Guarantees that products are paired with useful, compatible items — for example, phones with cases and chargers, laptops with sleeves and mice, and cameras with memory cards and tripods.
